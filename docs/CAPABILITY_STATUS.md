## Certification rule

A workflow cannot be promoted to executable/Gold merely because its UI flow or Gold-stage metadata exists. It needs dedicated domain analysis, evidence/validation gates, human review, authorized mailing, tracking, and proof/audit integration.

The current credit-report analysis is deliberately conservative and grounded in user-supplied/source text. The other three workflows remain catalog/UX definitions until their domain adapters are implemented and regression-tested.
